# Moral Turpitude Lookup — Phase 2 Batch 2 research (2026-10-12)

**Scope:** California criminal witness impeachment, not immigration CIMT or professional-license discipline.

## Critical legal rule
*People v. Castro* (1985) 38 Cal.3d 301 analyzes felony-conviction impeachment using the least adjudicated elements. *People v. Wheeler* (1992) 4 Cal.4th 284, 295–300 allows independently proven misconduct involving moral turpitude to be considered for impeachment under Evidence Code §352, but a misdemeanor conviction record offered to prove the conduct faces a hearsay objection. Do **not** treat a 'moral turpitude' characterization as automatic admissibility of the conviction document.
